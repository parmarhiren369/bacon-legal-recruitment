About Us · National Recruiting Firm

Experience. Reputation. Results.

Trusted legal placement you can count on across the United States.

Founded and led by attorneys and industry experts, we understand that the right candidate is the perfect mix of experience, skill and compatibility. We have a national network of talent and recruit in all markets.

At Bacon Legal Recruiting, we know that your team drives your success. We specialize in identifying and attracting the very top attorneys, State Lobbyists, and Federal Lobbyists for law firms and corporations across the United States. We have a skilled and client friendly team motivated to achieve excellence.

We recruit directly from the “passive” workforce. These are attorneys in the top of the talent pool that are not job searching. We do not use job boards as the overall talent pool on job boards has a disproportionately high percentage of lower performers.

As an attorney of more than 25 years himself, our founder and CEO, Kevin Bacon, knows the industry intricately from the inside out. His reputation in the legal and political world is impeccable, and he excels at finding the perfect fit for each role.
